Final grades

Each student's general weighted average for the school year, computed from the grades saved in every subject.

    GWA = Σ (Final Grade × Units) ÷ Σ Units
    Each subject's grade is weighted by its units. 75.00 and above is Passed. A student with an ungraded subject is marked Incomplete.
