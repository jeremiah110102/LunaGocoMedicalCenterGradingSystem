Tagging irregular students

An irregular student doesn't take every subject. On that student's row for a subject they don't take, either:

  • leave the subject or unit blank, or
  • write IRREG (also Irreg, IRREGULAR or Iregular, any capitals) in the subject code, subject or unit.
