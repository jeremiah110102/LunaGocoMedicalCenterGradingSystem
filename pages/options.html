Final grade (GWA)

Loading…

The scale used for the final grade on Final grades, student reports, emails and the Excel grade sheets. The GWA is always computed from the subjects' grades; it can then be shown on another scale, e.g. grades entered as 0–100 with the final grade as 1.00–5.00. You can change this at any time: nothing saved changes.

Audit trail

Loading…

The Audit trail records who added, edited or deleted what, with the old and new values. Choose how long entries are kept.

days

Old entries are deleted when an administrator opens the system (at most twice a day). A note of each delete is kept in the audit trail.

Combined classes

Loading…

Sometimes one teacher handles the same subject for two or more sections together (for example GE 1 for BSPT 1 and BSPH 1). Create one grading assignment per section as usual; with this on, Enter grades also shows them as one combined class list. Each grade is still saved to the student's own section, so every section's records, report cards and Excel sheets stay correct.

Term deadlines

Loading…

After a term's deadline, teachers can no longer add grades for that term's classes (grading assignments with that term). Saved grades stay; teachers can still ask for a grade change, and the registrar and administrators can still enter grades. Leave a date empty to keep the term open.

When a student changes section

Moving a student to another section (Students → Edit, or Move to section) can also move them in grading assignments: they're added to the new section's grading assignments for the same school year, and removed from the old section's ones where they have no saved grade yet. Saved grades are never deleted.
